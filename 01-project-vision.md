# Project Vision

## Mission
Build an independent, student-first information platform that presents verified information, official documents, timelines, and public reactions without endorsing political parties.

## Success Metrics
- Fast loading
- Accessible
- Transparent sourcing
- Every factual claim traceable to a source
- Clear distinction between Facts, Official Statements, Analysis, and Community Voices.

## Non-goals
- No political endorsements.
- No unverified rumors.
- No calls for unlawful activity.
